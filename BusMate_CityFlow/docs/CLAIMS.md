# CityFlow claims policy

## Allowed claims

- CityFlow is a scenario-based planning prototype using the assumptions and corridor labels in its configuration.
- Results describe the configured data, evaluation protocol, and model output only.
- Capacity, cost, fleet, and service settings are assumptions when the source data does not establish them.
- Accessibility and language settings are stored by browser origin; they carry between dashboards only when both are served from the same origin.

## Banned claims

- Do not claim measured Pune ridership, observed crowding, real PMPML improvement, or emissions saved.
- Do not claim WCAG compliance or that the fixed plan is the real timetable.
- Do not claim that settings carry between dashboards unless both are served from the same origin.

## Banned words

- proves
- publishable
- top 1%
- sub-50 ms
- guaranteed
- A+
